# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ski shoppers who need to browse, compare, add products to a cart, and prepare for checkout. Checkout and payment will be introduced in a later phase.

## Product Purpose

SkiiShop is a small product catalogue backed by an ASP.NET Core API. Success means a shopper can move from catalogue to product detail and cart with clear feedback, while the codebase stays simple enough to extend with checkout and authenticated administration later.

## Brand Personality

Clear, practical, confident. The interface should feel like a focused shop floor: useful information first, calm visual hierarchy, and small moments of motion that clarify interaction.

## Anti-references

Avoid dashboard-card mosaics, noisy gradients, excessive rounded containers, decorative animation, and marketing copy that hides product information.

## Design Principles

- Make product discovery the primary surface.
- Keep API state visible through useful loading, empty, and error feedback.
- Prefer simple feature boundaries over premature global state.
- Let imagery and price/name hierarchy do the work.
- Keep motion brief and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical WCAG AA fundamentals: semantic landmarks, keyboard-accessible controls, visible focus states, readable contrast, responsive layouts, descriptive image alternatives, and reduced-motion support.
